Adhan is a free and open-source Android app for prayer times, Adhan notifications and Qibla direction.

Features:
• Accurate prayer-time calculations with multiple calculation methods
• Offline-first astronomical calculation
• Qibla compass and distance to the Kaaba
• Local Adhan notifications and alarms
• Optional custom Adhan audio
• Manual location selection or device location
• No ads
• No analytics or tracking
• No account required
• No Google Play Services or Firebase dependency

The app is designed to keep essential prayer-time functionality available without proprietary services.
